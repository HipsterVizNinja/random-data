# Missouri High School Mascot Taxonomy (3-Tier)

Built from a verified sample of 100 Missouri public high schools (see `missouri_high_schools_mascots.csv`), spanning the St. Louis, Kansas City, Springfield, Columbia/Jefferson City, and Southeast Missouri regions.

## Tier 1: Animals
Living, non-human creatures — the dominant category (~60% of sampled mascots).

| Tier 2 | Tier 3 (Mascot) | Example School(s) |
|---|---|---|
| Big Cats & Wildcats | Tigers, Wildcats, Bobcats, Cougars, Blackcats, Bearcats | Kirksville, Blue Springs, Bowling Green, Affton, Fredericktown, Dexter |
| Canines | Wolves, Wolverines, Greyhounds, Bulldogs, Houn' Dawgs | Reeds Spring, Vashon, Clayton, Odessa, Aurora |
| Birds of Prey | Eagles, Falcons, Hawks, Owls | Grain Valley, Rockwood Summit, Clopton, Windsor |
| Songbirds & Poultry | Blue Jays, Jays, Cardinals, Orioles, Roosters | Liberty, Jefferson City, South Shelby, Concordia, Pleasant Hill |
| Farm & Hoofed Animals | Mules, Bisons, Rams, Mustangs, Broncos/Bronchos, Colts, Pintos | Poplar Bluff, Buffalo, Ladue, Eldon, Bayless |
| Other Mammals | Lions, Bears, Bruins, Jaguars | University City, Butler, Rock Bridge, Blue Springs South |

---

**Notes**
- "Pirates" appears frequently and is classified under Civic/Community Identity (historical seafaring figures) rather than Animals, consistent with its human-outlaw origin.
- "Midgets" (Putnam County) and "Huskers" (Lafayette County) are legacy/community-identity names tied to local history (mining heritage, agricultural identity) rather than literal animals or warriors.
- Full mascot-to-category mapping for every one of the 100 sampled schools is in the `Tier 1 Category` / `Tier 2 Category` columns of `missouri_high_schools_mascots.csv`.
