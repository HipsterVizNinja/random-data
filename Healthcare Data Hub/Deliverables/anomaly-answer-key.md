# Anomaly answer key

**Do not hand this to a demo audience before the reveal.** It gives away every planted defect and its magnitude.

This file is generated by `Build/build_docs.py` from `anomaly-manifest.json`, which the build itself produces. Every figure below was measured on the data that shipped, so the answer key cannot drift from the dataset.

**Scale:** demo — 48,000 members  
**Seed:** 20260911  
**Anomalies applied:** True

The companion document, [data-trust-validation.md](data-trust-validation.md), is the client-facing artifact: it proves the defects were found and controlled without giving away how. This one is the teaching artifact.

## A9a — Duplicate claim lines from a re-driven extract

2,840 claim lines from service month 2024-07 were loaded twice under batch BATCH-REDRIVE-20240714. The surrogate primary key claim_line_key is unique across all of them, so a primary-key uniqueness test passes. The business key (claim_number, claim_line_number, adjudication_seq) is violated.

**Naive error**

- **measure**: paid amount for service month 2024-07
- **correct**: 17347289.86
- **naive**: 19345434.31
- **overstatement_dollars**: 1998144.45
- **overstatement_pct**: 11.52

**Rows added:** 2,840

**Affected service month:** 2024-07

**How to detect it**

Test grain uniqueness on the BUSINESS key, never the surrogate. Then group by source_load_batch_id to find the offending load.

**How to control for it**

De-duplicate on (claim_number, claim_line_number, adjudication_seq), keeping the earliest load batch.

## A12 — Orphan servicing providers, concentrated at one site

1,312 claim lines carry a servicing_provider_master_id with no row in the credentialing source - locum tenens and out-of-network fill-ins. 68% of them sit at UC-NORTHGATE, which staffs through an agency.

**Naive error**

- **measure**: paid amount silently dropped by an inner join to the provider dimension
- **dropped_dollars**: 462294.91
- **site_paid_all_lines**: 2245667.77
- **site_paid_after_inner_join**: 2059439.9
- **site_understatement_pct**: 8.29

**Rows affected:** 1,312

**Concentration site:** UC-NORTHGATE

**How to detect it**

Anti-join every foreign key before using it, and report the orphan count AND the orphan dollars. Never inner join a fact to a dimension without first counting what the join would drop.

**How to control for it**

Resolve unmatched keys to the -1 Unknown dimension member and carry provider_resolution_status so the defect stays countable.

3,782 referral orders (4.0%) have no referring provider recorded. Grouping leakage by referring provider silently excludes them.

**Rows affected:** 3,782

## A1 — Referral auto-close rule at one site

A scheduling rule effective 2024-07-01 auto-closes ORTHO-NR referrals at day 30 whether or not care happened. Closure rate and open rate both stop meaning anything at that site.

**Measured**

- **day30_closure_share_post_rule**: 0.6454
- **day30_closure_share_pre_rule**: 0.0312
- **day30_closure_share_other_sites**: 0.0315
- **system_actor_share_at_site**: 0.6316
- **confirmation_rate_at_site**: 0.3788
- **confirmation_rate_other_sites**: 0.7224
- **open_rate_at_site**: 0.0412
- **open_rate_other_sites**: 0.1889

**How to detect it**

Plot time-to-status before trusting any status field. A spike at a round number is a workflow rule, not clinical behavior. Then check closure_actor_type.

**How to control for it**

Measure referral outcome against a confirming event, not against the status field. Confirm through claims AND appointments so the finding survives a challenge to either join.

## A10 — Reversal netting, including orphan reversals

118 reversals reference an original that adjudicated before this window opened, so there is nothing here to net against. They are retained and flagged.

**Measured**

- **correct_paid_all_rows**: 611525380.08
- **correct_paid_current_version**: 611475423.53
- **two_formulas_agree**: False
- **orphan_reversal_dollars**: -146028.09
- **naive_seq1_overstatement_pct**: 0.65
- **naive_positive_only_overstatement_pct**: 5.8
- **naive_drop_all_reversals_error_pct**: 0.02

**How to detect it**

Check that both sanctioned nettings tie. Anti-join reversals to their originals.

**How to control for it**

Sum every row, or sum WHERE is_current_version. Do NOT add a reversal exclusion: orphan reversals are current and negative on purpose, and excluding them breaks the tie.

## A3 — EHR referral directory is a stale copy of network status

The directory is maintained separately from the contract and nobody updates it. Summit Point's contract terminated 2024-10-01 and the pick-list still reads PAR. A governance failure with a price tag, and nobody made a bad clinical decision.

**Measured**

- **sites_where_directory_disagrees_with_contract**: 1
- **detail**
  - {'site_code': 'ASC-SUMMIT', 'referral_network_status': 'PAR', 'network_status': 'NONPAR', 'last_maintained_date': '2024-08-15'}
- **referrals_sent_to_those_sites**: 9743

**How to detect it**

Join the directory to the contract and diff the status columns. Then check last_maintained_date - a stale date on a field that drives routing is a finding on its own.

**How to control for it**

Never treat a directory field as a measure. Derive network status from the contract with an as-of-service-date join.

## A4 — Crosswalk under-match, and it is NOT random

A share of EHR records never resolve to a member. The loss skews toward ORTHO-NR patients, whose registration workflow does not capture the subscriber ID. So a naive inner join deletes part of the headline finding and UNDERSTATES the leakage - the careless analyst gets a wrong answer that still looks plausible, which is the worst kind.

**Measured**

- **match_tier_shares**
  - **EXACT**: 0.817
  - **DETERMINISTIC_NAME_DOB_ZIP**: 0.1329
  - **UNMATCHED**: 0.0311
  - **PROBABILISTIC**: 0.019
- **skew**
  - **unmatched_rate_at_site**: 0.0557
  - **unmatched_rate_elsewhere**: 0.0271
  - **skew_factor**: 2.05

**How to detect it**

Profile the unmatched population against the matched on several attributes before reporting ANY cross-source rate. If it is skewed, every rate built on an inner join is biased in a direction you have not measured.

**How to control for it**

Left join, resolve unmatched to the -1 Unknown member, and report that bucket rather than dropping it.

## A5 — Crosswalk over-match: two people wearing one identity

Twins and Jr/Sr pairs matched on name, date of birth and address. They surface as artificial super-utilizers at the very top of the cost distribution, contaminating precisely the top-1% figure the demo quotes. Over-matching is the more interesting failure and is almost never demonstrated.

**Measured**

- **master_persons_collapsing_multiple_people**: 33
- **found_by_sex_mismatch_alone**: 18
- **found_only_by_date_of_birth**: 15
- **collapsed_persons_in_top_1pct_of_cost**: 7
- **their_allowed_amount**: 8364357.45
- **top_1pct_threshold_allowed**: 459227.87

**How to detect it**

Within each master_person_id, count distinct sex and distinct date of birth. Sex mismatch is the cheapest detector but it only catches mixed-sex pairs; same-sex twins need the date-of-birth check.

**How to control for it**

Exclude or split PROBABILISTIC matches below a score threshold, and report counts at both thresholds so the sensitivity is visible rather than hidden.

## A6 — Retroactive attribution restatement

High-cost members are retro-terminated from attribution in exactly the months containing an inpatient stay at a non-affiliated hospital. Their cost leaves the numerator and their member-months leave the denominator, so PMPM improves without any care changing.

**Measured**

- **restated_member_months**: 784
- **retro_terminated_members**: 167
- **retro_terminated_member_months**: 224
- **ordinary_churn_member_months**: 560

**How to detect it**

Compare roster VERSIONS, not just the current roster. Hold a cohort fixed and re-run the measure.

**How to control for it**

Report PMPM on a fixed cohort alongside the open-panel figure, and state the as-of date of the roster used.

## A8 — Overlapping coverage spans, concentrated in one group

COBRA running alongside active coverage, and an acquisition where the prior group span was never terminated. Summing span lengths double counts every overlap, and because the overlap concentrates in one employer group the error is UNEVEN: that group's PMPM reads better than truth. A number that is wrong unevenly is the kind that survives review.

**Measured**

- **members_with_overlap**: 1680
- **overlap_share_of_members**: 0.035
- **reasons**
  - **COBRA overlapping active coverage**: 1310
  - **Prior group span never terminated at acquisition**: 370

**How to detect it**

Test member-month grain uniqueness, and compare the de-duplicated union against the naive span sum BY GROUP, not just in total. The total hides it.

**How to control for it**

Build the denominator as a gaps-and-islands union of coverage. Never compute member-months from raw spans.

## A9b — Legitimate lines that look like duplicates

Bilateral procedures and same-day repeats produce near-identical rows distinguished ONLY by modifier. De-duplicating on 'the obvious business columns' destroys them and UNDERSTATES paid. This runs in the opposite direction to A9a, and the naive fix for one creates the other.

**Measured**

- **lines_distinguished_only_by_modifier**: 30460
- **paid_at_risk_if_wrongly_deduplicated**: 25517881.28
- **modifier_mix**
  - **50**: 9031
  - **RT**: 7221
  - **LT**: 7113
  - **76**: 7095

**How to detect it**

Before de-duplicating, check whether the candidate rows differ on modifier. If they do, they are distinct services.

**How to control for it**

Modifiers are part of line identity. Include them in the key.

## A11a — Encounter type encoded differently by site, one value unmapped

Most sites emit OFFICE, four emit OFFICE VISIT, and one emits AMB - which has no row in the crosswalk seed at all. It lands as UNKNOWN and gets filtered out as junk unless someone profiles distinct values by source.

**Measured**

- **value_counts**
  - **OFFICE**: 316337
  - **OFFICE VISIT**: 164214
  - **URGENT**: 36880
  - **ED**: 23782
  - **AMB**: 16755
  - **INPATIENT**: 8609
- **unmapped_value**: AMB
- **unmapped_share_of_ambulatory**: 0.0337

**How to detect it**

Profile distinct values of every low-cardinality column BY SOURCE and BY MONTH. The month cut is what turns a mystery into a visible step change.

**How to control for it**

Assert conformance COVERAGE: every distinct source value must have a mapping. That one check catches this class of defect everywhere it occurs.

## A11b — Sex encoded differently in every source system

Eligibility emits M/F/U, the EHR emits HL7 table 0001 values 1/2/9, and the attribution feed emits Male/Female/Unknown. Conforming them is a prerequisite for any demographic cut.

**Measured**

- **encodings**
  - **MERIDIAN_ELIG**
    - M
    - F
    - U
  - **CARELINE_EHR**
    - 1
    - 2
    - 9
  - **NORTHLAKE_VBC**
    - Male
    - Female
    - Unknown

**How to detect it**

Profile distinct values of every low-cardinality column BY SOURCE.

**How to control for it**

One conformed dimension with a documented source-of-truth per attribute.

## A7 — Claims runout on the most recent service months

Claims absent from this extract are exactly the ones that had not adjudicated by the paid-through date of 2026-01-02 - the slowest-paying, not a random sample. Retention below is measured against each month's own natural volume, which is the only denominator that makes the designed and realized figures comparable.
