Allowed PMPM by service month

Attributed roster · 2024-01 → 2025-12
Allowed PMPM, as reported Completion-adjusted (not scored)

The whole reason this app exists. Read naively, PMPM falls from $1,035 in March 2025 to $444 in December — a 53% "improvement". Three things cause it and only one is care: runout (Oct–Dec are 78% / 54% / 31% complete), roster restatement (5–11 points of monthly improvement, §8 of the plan), and real performance. Months right of the shaded edge are displayed and never scored. The completion factor is for this chart and the accrual view only — grossing up an incomplete month and paying on it pays on a forecast.

Benchmark reconciliation

Why the scorecard is peer-relative. vbc_benchmark is calibrated against the whole book and tracks it closely. The fingerprint. 69.0% of North Ridge closures land in days 25–31 against 9.0% for everyone else, spiking exactly at day 30. A spike at a round number is a workflow rule, not clinical behaviour. days_to_closure is a stored column, so this histogram is one drag from the leakage view — which is what makes the finding earned rather than lucky.

Closure rate Confirmed-event rate Closed by SYSTEM

Three series, one 0–100% scale — never a dual axis. The month the rule turns on, closure steps 82.5% → 96.9% while the confirmed-event rate drops 80.5% → 56.5% and closure_actor_type = 'SYSTEM' goes 0% → 65.2%. Two independent confirmations exist — no appointment and no professional claim — which is what kills the first skeptic's "your match rate is bad" objection.

Referring providers — North Ridge

n ≥ 30 referrals · PY2025 YTD

Read this as exoneration, not indictment. All 30 scoreable referrers sit between 54.8% and 66.7% true out-of-network. That is not a few bad actors — it is one pick-list nobody owned, and the auto-close rule was a reasonable local fix by someone trying to clear a worklist. The villain is the absence of architecture, never the client's staff — and in an incentive app, where the output is a cheque, getting that framing backwards is the one thing here that damages a relationship.

INPUT_PAYOUT_DECISION

Editable · adjustment reason required when approved ≠ modelled

Why the definition is a stored column. Network Definition Used makes the difference between the two scorecards auditable after the fact. Without it, a payout approved in March and a payout approved in June can differ by $259,141 for reasons nobody can reconstruct — which is the failure this whole dataset is about, arriving in the ledger instead of the dashboard.

The appeals mechanism

This is what keeps Priya at the table.
